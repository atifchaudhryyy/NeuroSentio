Our Values

What drives us

One belief guides everything we build: everyone deserves a workplace that understands and supports them.

  • 01

    Inclusive from the start

    We design for different minds first, not as an afterthought. If a feature adds stress or noise, it does not ship.

  • 02

    Built with the community

    Neurodivergent testers shape what we make. We listen, try ideas together and keep what genuinely helps.

  • 03

    Practical over perfect

    Small tools that work on a hard day beat clever features nobody uses. Every screen has one clear job.

  • 04

    Honest and private

    Plain language, no ads and no data selling. You always know what the app does and who can see what.

Our Story

Why NeuroSentio?

NeuroSentio started with a simple idea: work should feel better for everyone. Many neurodivergent people face daily challenges in workplaces that weren’t designed with their needs in mind. We saw the need for a practical, easy-to-use solution that brings together the right tools in one place, helping people feel more supported, focused and in control.

A More Inclusive Workplace

Built for real people,
grounded in real needs

Every feature is shaped by lived experience, from quieter screens to fewer decisions, so ordinary workdays feel easier to handle. We’re committed to building a more inclusive future where neurodivergent individuals feel understood, respected and empowered at work.
