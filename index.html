Why NeuroSentio

Workplace support that fits your brain

Busy offices, back-to-back tasks and unspoken rules can be draining. NeuroSentio is built around a few simple promises.

  • Fewer decisions

    Clear next steps and gentle defaults, so your energy goes into the work, not into working out where to start.

  • Calm by design

    Soft colours, quiet screens and no noisy streak shaming. Light and dark themes for every kind of day.

  • Your data, your rules

    No ads and no data selling. Your check-ins stay private unless you choose to share them.

  • Shaped by real people

    Designed and tested with neurodivergent people, so every feature solves a problem someone actually has.

What is NeuroSentio?

A free mobile app that helps neurodivergent people feel more supported, focused and in control at work. It brings together a planner, energy check-ins, an AI assistant, Neuro Cards and Safe Mode in one calm app.

How much does NeuroSentio cost?

NeuroSentio is currently free to download. If we ever add paid features, we’ll make the price clear before you pay anything.

Do I need a diagnosis to use it?

No. You don’t need a diagnosis, and the app never asks for one. If the tools help you, they’re for you.

Is my data private?

Yes. We collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your personal information, and your check-ins aren’t shared with your employer or anyone else unless you choose to share them. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Can NeuroSentio help in an emergency?

NeuroSentio is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999 or 112 in the UK (or your local emergency number). For urgent mental health support, call NHS 111 or Samaritans on 116 123.
